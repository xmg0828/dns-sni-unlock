# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code and behavior, not people.
- Assume good faith while still treating security and operational risk seriously.
- Respect privacy; never publish another person's credentials, IP addresses, or infrastructure details.
- Use the project only on networks and systems you are authorized to administer.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Encouraging abuse, open-proxy deployment, unauthorized access, or evasion of access controls.
- Deliberately submitting destructive code, hidden payloads, or unreviewable obfuscation.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile or GitHub's reporting tools. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Maintainers should handle reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and explain enforcement decisions when doing so is safe and appropriate.
